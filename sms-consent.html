SMS Consent (Required)

By submitting your phone number or texting START to +1-855-923-0032, you agree to receive SMS messages from DealDily SMS Notifications. Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase and is separate from any other consent (including Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy). Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

📌 Consent to receive SMS is NOT a condition of purchase. You can use DealDily products and services without subscribing to SMS notifications.

What You Are Consenting To

When you opt in to DealDily SMS Notifications, you are consenting to receive:

  • SMS reminders for appointments, tasks, bills, medications, and events you create
  • Account-related alerts and service notifications
  • Responses to HELP requests
  • Opt-out confirmation messages when you text STOP

What You Are NOT Consenting To

  • Marketing or promotional messages unrelated to your requested service
  • Sharing your number with third parties for marketing
  • Messages from other companies or services
  • Any messages after you text STOP

How to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent. This will not affect any purchases you have made.

Consent Records

We maintain records of all opt-in and opt-out requests as required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and carrier compliance requirements. These records are kept private and are not shared for marketing purposes.

Carriers & Compatibility

Our SMS service is available on all major US carriers. Carrier is not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile plan.
