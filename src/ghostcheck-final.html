FAQ

Real Questions.
Real Answers.

Why can't I just ask the landlord for more photos? +
Because landlords control the camera. They choose the angle, the lighting, and what's cropped out. Every photo they send you is a marketing asset — not a disclosure. GhostCheck sends someone who works for you, not the landlord. Our Ghosters are contractually prohibited from accepting tips, gifts, or bribes from property managers. What they find is what you get — unedited.
Can't I just ask a friend who lives nearby to check it out? +
You can — but your friend doesn't know what to look for. They won't measure square footage, test water pressure, check for mold behind fixtures, note noise levels at 8AM, or document findings in a format you can use to negotiate. Our Ghosters follow a 47-point inspection checklist. They're trained to find what landlords hide — and to document it in a way that holds up in a lease dispute.
What if the landlord refuses to let the Ghoster in? +
That's actually valuable information. If a landlord refuses an independent walkthrough before you sign, that's a significant red flag we include in your report. In those cases, our Ghoster performs an exterior audit — building condition, neighborhood assessment, noise levels, street safety, nearby amenities — and you get a full refund on the interior portion. Either way, you make a more informed decision than you would have otherwise.
How do I know the Ghoster isn't being paid by the landlord? +
Every Ghoster signs our Code of Conduct which explicitly prohibits accepting anything of value from landlords or property managers. Any Ghoster who violates this is immediately terminated — and we pay a $50 whistleblower bonus to any Ghoster who reports a violation. We also run a tamper-evident audit on every report: if a finding is missing from the checklist with no explanation, it triggers a review. Your report is generated by AI from raw field notes before any human editing happens.
