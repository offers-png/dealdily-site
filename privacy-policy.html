Privacy Policy

DealDily SMS Notifications · Last updated: January 2026

🔒 We do not sell, share, or rent your mobile number or personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

1. Who We Are

DealDily SMS Notifications ("DealDily," "we," "our," or "us") is operated by Saleh Saleh and provides user-requested SMS notifications, including reminders and account-related alerts. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our SMS service:

  • Mobile phone number — collected when you opt in to our SMS service
  • Reminder content — the text of reminders or notifications you request
  • Opt-in/opt-out records — required for compliance with carrier and regulatory requirements
  • Message delivery records — timestamps and delivery status of messages sent

We do not collect payment information through our SMS service. Payments are processed securely through Stripe.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • Sending you the SMS notifications and reminders you requested
  • Processing opt-in and opt-out requests
  • Providing customer support
  • Complying with legal and carrier requirements
  • Improving our service quality and reliability

4. SMS Data Sharing

We do not share your mobile phone number or SMS data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data with:

  • Twilio / Telnyx — our SMS delivery providers, solely to deliver your messages
  • Legal authorities — only if required by law or court order

5. Data Retention

We retain your mobile number and message history for as long as you remain subscribed to our SMS service. When you unsubscribe (reply STOP), we retain your number on our opt-out list to ensure we do not contact you again, as required by law.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Opt out — reply STOP to any message at any time
  • Access your data — email us to request a copy of your data
  • Delete your data — email us to request deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Get help — reply HELP to any message or email support@dealdily.com

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our SMS service is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by sending an SMS notification or by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
